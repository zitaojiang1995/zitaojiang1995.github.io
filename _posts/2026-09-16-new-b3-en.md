In September 2026, **nine undergraduate third-year students** from the Department of Living Environment Design joined our laboratory.

The students will develop their own research topics based on their individual interests and begin working on various research projects related to the built and urban environment.

We are delighted to welcome our new members and look forward to working and growing together as a laboratory.
