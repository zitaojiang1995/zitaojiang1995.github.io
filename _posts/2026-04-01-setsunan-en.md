I joined the Department of Living Environment Design, Faculty of Science and Engineering, [Setsunan University](https://www.setsunan.ac.jp/), as a Lecturer in April 2026.

My research and teaching will focus on building and urban environmental engineering, including airflow and natural ventilation, urban microclimate, building energy, and data-driven modeling.

I am also establishing the **Building & Urban Environmental Dynamics Laboratory**, where we will explore new approaches to understanding and improving environmental performance in buildings and cities.
