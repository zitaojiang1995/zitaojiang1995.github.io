<div class="research-intro">
  <p>
    My research explores <strong>airflow, heat, and environmental transport in buildings and cities</strong>,
    with the goal of creating healthier, more comfortable, and energy-efficient built environments.
  </p>

  <p>
    I combine <strong>computational fluid dynamics (CFD), wind-tunnel experiments, field measurements,
    environmental sensing, and data-driven modeling</strong> to understand environmental processes across scales,
    from airflow through a building opening to thermal environments at the urban scale.
  </p>

  <p>
    A central direction of my research is to connect <strong>physical understanding with prediction and application</strong>:
    understanding environmental phenomena through experiments and simulation, developing fast predictive models,
    and ultimately applying them to building design, environmental control, and climate-responsive cities.
  </p>
</div>


<div class="research-section" id="building-airflow-and-natural-ventilation">

  <div class="research-section-number">01</div>

  <h2>Building Airflow &amp; Natural Ventilation</h2>

  <p>
    Natural ventilation can reduce building energy use and improve indoor environmental quality,
    but its performance is strongly affected by fluctuating wind conditions, surrounding buildings,
    opening configurations, and indoor geometry.
  </p>

  <p>
    My research investigates the <strong>physical mechanisms governing wind- and buoyancy-driven ventilation</strong>,
    from airflow through openings to whole-building ventilation and contaminant transport.
  </p>

  <p>
    I combine <strong>wind-tunnel experiments, tracer-gas measurements, and CFD simulations</strong>
    to characterize ventilation processes and develop practical prediction methods.
  </p>

  <h3>Current research</h3>

  <ul>
    <li>Wind- and buoyancy-driven natural ventilation</li>
    <li>Cross and single-sided ventilation</li>
    <li>Wind pressure and ventilation-rate prediction</li>
    <li>Effects of surrounding buildings and urban configurations</li>
    <li>Local ventilation effectiveness and contaminant transport</li>
    <li>Ventilation of vehicles and semi-enclosed spaces</li>
  </ul>

  <div class="research-method-tags">
    <span>CFD</span>
    <span>Wind Tunnel</span>
    <span>Tracer Gas</span>
    <span>Ventilation Measurement</span>
  </div>

</div>


<div class="research-section" id="urban-microclimate-and-outdoor-thermal-environment">

  <div class="research-section-number">02</div>

  <h2>Urban Microclimate &amp; Outdoor Environment</h2>

  <p>
    Cities create highly heterogeneous thermal environments through interactions among
    <strong>urban morphology, solar radiation, vegetation, water bodies, surface materials, and airflow</strong>.
  </p>

  <p>
    Understanding these interactions is increasingly important for climate adaptation
    and the design of comfortable and resilient outdoor spaces.
  </p>

  <p>
    My research combines <strong>field measurements, mobile environmental sensing,
    urban-scale modeling, and spatial analysis</strong> to investigate how urban form
    influences pedestrian-scale environmental conditions.
  </p>

  <h3>Current and emerging research</h3>

  <ul>
    <li>Urban thermal environments and pedestrian thermal comfort</li>
    <li>River and waterfront cooling effects</li>
    <li>Mobile measurement of thermal environment and air quality</li>
    <li>Urban morphology and solar exposure</li>
    <li>Microclimate–building interactions</li>
    <li>Climate-responsive urban design</li>
  </ul>

  <div class="research-highlight">
    <strong>Southeast Asia</strong><br>
    I am particularly interested in extending this research to
    <strong>cities and buildings in Southeast Asia</strong>, where hot and humid climates,
    rapid urbanization, and intensive cooling demand create important opportunities
    for climate-responsive architecture and urban environmental design.
  </div>

  <div class="research-method-tags">
    <span>Field Measurement</span>
    <span>Mobile Sensing</span>
    <span>Urban Analysis</span>
    <span>Microclimate</span>
  </div>

</div>


<div class="research-section" id="data-driven-building-physics">

  <div class="research-section-number">03</div>

  <h2>Data-driven Building Physics</h2>

  <p>
    Detailed CFD simulations provide rich information about airflow and thermal environments,
    but they remain computationally expensive for many design, optimization,
    and real-time control applications.
  </p>

  <p>
    My research develops <strong>reduced-order and machine-learning models that retain
    physical information while enabling rapid prediction</strong>.
  </p>

  <p>
    By combining CFD databases, physical constraints, and sparse sensor measurements,
    I aim to bridge the gap between high-fidelity environmental simulation
    and real-time building applications.
  </p>

  <h3>Research directions</h3>

  <ul>
    <li>Proper Orthogonal Decomposition (POD)</li>
    <li>Reduced-order modeling</li>
    <li>Deep Operator Networks (DeepONet)</li>
    <li>Physics-informed machine learning</li>
    <li>Sparse-sensor reconstruction of environmental fields</li>
    <li>Surrogate models for rapid environmental prediction</li>
    <li>Digital twins and real-time building environmental control</li>
  </ul>

  <div class="research-flow">

    <div class="research-flow-item">

      CFD / Experiments / Measurements

    <div class="research-flow-arrow">→</div>

    </div>

    <div class="research-flow-arrow">→</div>

<div class="research-section" id="building-energy-and-climate-responsive-design">

  <div class="research-section-number">04</div>

  <h2>Building Energy &amp; Climate-responsive Design</h2>

  <p>
    Building energy performance is closely connected to the surrounding climate,
    urban microclimate, ventilation strategy, and environmental control.
  </p>

  <p>
    As an emerging direction of my research, I am extending airflow and microclimate studies
    toward <strong>whole-building environmental and energy performance</strong>.
  </p>

  <h3>Research interests</h3>

  <ul>
    <li>Natural ventilation potential and energy-saving effects</li>
    <li>Climate-responsive building operation</li>
    <li>Building energy simulation</li>
    <li>Microclimate–building energy interactions</li>
    <li>Coupling airflow prediction with building performance simulation</li>
    <li>Environmental sensing and building control</li>
  </ul>

  <div class="research-method-tags">
    <span>Building Energy Simulation</span>
