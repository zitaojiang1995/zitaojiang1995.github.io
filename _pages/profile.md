# Profile

## Biography

Zitao Jiang is a researcher in building and urban environmental engineering, with research interests spanning natural ventilation, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), wind engineering, urban microclimate, and data-driven modeling.

His research combines numerical simulation, wind tunnel experiments, field measurements, and reduced-order/data-driven approaches to understand and predict airflow and environmental performance in buildings and cities.

## Research Development

### Natural Ventilation & Indoor Airflow
Wind-driven and buoyancy-driven natural ventilation, single-sided and cross ventilation, turbulence effects, and indoor airflow.

### Wind Engineering & Urban Environment
Building aerodynamics, pedestrian wind environments, urban airflow, pollutant dispersion, and integration of CFD with wind tunnel experiments.

### Urban Microclimate & Environmental Measurement
Field and mobile measurements of outdoor thermal environments, urban morphology, waterfront cooling, solar exposure, and climate-responsive urban design.

### Data-driven Building Physics
Reduced-order modeling, POD, DeepONet, sparse-sensor reconstruction, physics-informed machine learning, and real-time environmental prediction.

### Building Energy & Environmental Control
Integration of airflow prediction with building energy simulation, natural ventilation potential, environmental sensing, and real-time building control.
