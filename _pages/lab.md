# Research Group

Our research focuses on the **building and urban environment**, combining numerical simulation, experiments, field measurements, and data-driven modeling.

We are particularly interested in connecting fundamental understanding of airflow and heat transfer with practical problems in buildings and cities.

## Research Topics

### 🌬️ Building Airflow & Ventilation

Natural ventilation, indoor airflow, contaminant transport, and ventilation effectiveness.

### 🌇 Urban Environment

Urban microclimate, outdoor thermal environment, waterfront cooling, and pedestrian environmental quality.

### 💻 Computational Building Physics

CFD, reduced-order modeling, machine learning, DeepONet, and physics-informed modeling.

### 📡 Environmental Measurement

Wind-tunnel experiments, field measurements, mobile sensing, and development of new environmental measurement systems.

## Facilities & Methods

Our research uses a combination of:

- Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
- Wind-tunnel experiments
- Environmental sensors
- Tracer-gas measurement
- Mobile measurement systems
- Building and urban environmental data analysis
